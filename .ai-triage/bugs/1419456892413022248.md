## Root cause
CONFIRMED: Content.Shared/_DV/Carrying/CarryingSystem.cs:325–332 (`Drop`) removes `KnockedDownComponent` and calls `_standingState.Stand(carried)` unconditionally on every drop; for a player asleep (nocturine) this erases the sleep-induced knockdown while `SleepingComponent` remains, so the sleeper regains movement control. Causal chain matches reporter's steps 2–4 exactly.

## Proposed fix (do NOT implement!)
In `Drop()`: only `RemComp<KnockedDownComponent>` when the knockdown was added by carrying (e.g. tag/flag the comp in `Carry()` or check `carried` has no `SleepingComponent`/`MobState` crit and no other knockdown source); call `_standingState.Stand()` only if the entity is conscious (guard with `MobStateSystem.IsConscious` / `TryComp<SleepingComponent>`). Why: preserves sleep-induced downed state and the TODO's no-legs case.
