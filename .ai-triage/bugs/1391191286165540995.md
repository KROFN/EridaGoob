## Root cause
HYPOTHESIS (Medium, partially confirmed): the footprint cleaning pipeline preserves the spilled liquid — FootprintCleanEvent → FootprintSystem.ToPuddle clones the footprint solution and TrySpillAt re-creates a slippery puddle on the same tile, and 50u tile overflow in FootprintInteraction already spawns real puddles mid-trail. Since SpaceLube never evaporates, lube trails keep converting footprints↔puddles: slip while cleaning cancels the mop DoAfter (BreakOnMove), so the trail can be effectively unerasesble. The Footprint entity itself has no SlipperyComponent (Resources/Prototypes/_White/Entities/Effects/puddle.yml lines 5-35, only slipFixture) — the slip comes from the spawned puddles [SRC + INFER].

## Proposed fix (do NOT implement!)
In FootprintSystem.OnFootprintClean/ToPuddle: transfer the footprint solution into the cleaning entity (absorbent) instead of re-spilling, or restrict the event to dry cleaning reagents (SpaceCleaner); optionally make residual lube puddles evaporate or cap per-tile accumulation before spill.
